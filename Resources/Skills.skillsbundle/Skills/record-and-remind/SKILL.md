---
name: record-and-remind
description: Use when someone wants to record a show or channel, schedule or cancel a recording, stop one, find saved recordings, or be reminded when a programme starts.
---
A programme is better than a clock time. When the person names a show, find it with the guide search
tool first and pass that programme to the record programme tool or the reminder tool. Use the
schedule recording tool, with a channel and times, only when they give a time rather than a show,
or the guide has no entry.

Record channel starts now and opens the app, because iOS only lets a recording begin from the
foreground. Mention that once if the person asked for it while doing something else.

A scheduled recording only runs if the app is alive at its start time. Within six hours the app stays
awake by itself. Further out, iOS sends a notification asking the person to open the app. Say this when
scheduling something more than six hours away, so nobody loses a recording to a surprise.

If the person asks what is coming up, use the recordings list tool. For saved files use the saved
recordings tool, then the play, share or delete recording tools.

Cancelling a scheduled recording and deleting a saved one cannot be undone. Those tools ask for
confirmation themselves. Do not ask a second time, and never delete more than the one named.

Stopping keeps what was already recorded. Say so if the person sounds worried about losing it.
