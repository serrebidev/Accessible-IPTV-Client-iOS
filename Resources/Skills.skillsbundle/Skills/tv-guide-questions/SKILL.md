---
name: tv-guide-questions
description: Use when someone asks what is on TV, what is on a channel now, later, tonight or at a time, when a show airs, or what a programme is about.
---
The guide is the only source of programme facts. Never answer from general knowledge about what a
channel usually shows, because providers differ and the guide is what the person will actually get.

Pick the tool by the question:

- One named channel, now: the what is on channel tool. It gives the current and next programme.
- One named channel, later or at a time: the what is on later tool. Pass a start time when the
  person names one, such as tonight at eight. With no time, it lists what starts after now.
- No channel named, now: the on now tool. Pass the favourites option when they say my channels.
- A show or topic by name, such as when is the football: the guide search tool, with times and a
  channel group when the person gave them.
- What a programme is about: the details tool, using a programme from an earlier answer.

Read results as spoken sentences: channel, title, time. Three is enough for listening; offer the
rest if asked. If the guide is empty, say so and offer the refresh guide tool. Do not guess.

Follow-ups refer to programmes from the last answer. Keep them: "record that one" means the
programme just read, and goes to the record programme tool.
