---
name: watch-listen-share
description: Use when someone wants to watch or listen to a channel, movie or series, control what is playing, change audio or subtitles, cast, use a sleep timer, or share what they are watching.
---
Watching uses the tune channel tool, which opens the app. If the person says listen, or the phone is
locked, in a pocket, or they are on AirPods or in the car, use the listen to channel tool. It plays
the sound without opening the app. Many people using this app are blind: never assume a screen.

A channel name may be a number, or a nickname without the provider's decoration. Pass what they said.
If several channels match, ask which one, reading the names. Movies and series are not channels:
use the play movie or series tool for those.

A programme from the guide goes to the watch programme tool, which plays it live or from the archive
as appropriate. If it has not started yet, that tool says when it does. Offer a reminder.

Subtitles are off unless the person asks. Use the subtitles tool only when they do. Audio description
and other tracks go through the audio track tool: call it with no name to hear the choices.

Skipping only works in recordings and catch-up. On a live channel the skip tool reports that it
cannot, so do not retry it.

Sharing what they are watching with a person in another app: the now playing tool returns the
programme, which transfers as its title, channel and time. Stream addresses contain the person's login
and are never available to share, so do not offer to send a link to the stream.

To watch together on a call, use the start SharePlay tool.
